# 01 / Architecture concept
**Working title: The Terraced Frame.** A calm residential volume defined by a consistent structural rhythm, deep window recesses and a small number of upper-level setbacks.

This is an original conceptual design for a digital study. It is not the old Afrah tower, a surveyed site or a construction proposal. The site and all dimensions originate in the new brief or the explicitly documented adjustments.

Design intent: a grounded lower body; a sheltered arrival; glazing recessed behind stone; upper terraces that interrupt the silhouette without twisting geometry. Daylight catches reveals; dusk exposes depth through varied internal light.
Main website opportunity: a single footprint can become structure, become façade and then change atmosphere without breaking spatial continuity.
Massing options and tradeoffs appear in 03; B is recommended but not represented as user-approved.
