# 08 / Asset research
Reference roles and source evidence are in [the architecture board](../research/architecture/architecture-reference-board.md). Research concerns proportions, façade rhythm, lighting and camera framing; no source building is copied.

Search priorities:
1. Neutral outdoor day/dusk environments with compatible horizons.
2. Warm, low-contrast stone without enormous tiled veins.
3. Small set of deciduous trees with varied crowns and useful LODs.
4. Restrained paving and sheltered timber.
5. Later interior detail footage from the same visual family.

See [the exact shopping/search list](../assets/ASSET_SEARCH_LIST.md). No speculative catalogue download is recorded as an acquired asset. Original building and site are authored assets. Reference photographs are evidence or explicitly temporary study media, never described as the finished Afrah building.
