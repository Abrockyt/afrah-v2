Reveal groups overlap but keep the ground fixed. Use vertical masks or progressive visibility, not a whole-building scale animation. Slabs do not fly in from the sky.
Allow a short settled interval at 1.0 before light changes. Native chapter headings have their own brief opacity fades; do not animate every letter.
Scrubbing is reversible and deterministic. Replay resets timeline safely, maintains loading cache and returns camera to the start. Use the same IDs/materials for future floor interaction.
