# 04 / Recommended massing
**B / Stepped Terraces — proposed selection, pending review.**

G + L01–L08 retain the 46×28m envelope. L09 steps to 42×24m, L10 to 38×20m, and the premium L11 to 34×16m. Symmetric initial setbacks make the schematic easy to evaluate; later orientation/daylight study may redistribute them.
Top height 42.45m includes the roof/parapet allowance. Keep the structural bay rhythm continuous where loads permit; never describe an unresolved cantilever as engineered.

Preserve in all later work: single coherent body, three crown steps, deep façade shadow, sheltered ground arrival, restrained roof equipment.
Review with the supplied front, side, aerial and street views. Revise massing before detailed façade work if the crown feels compressed or the lower volume too broad.
