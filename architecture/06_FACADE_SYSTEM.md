# 06 / Façade system
Proposed rhythm: ten broad modules across 46m, approximately 4.6m per module before corner/structural adjustments. Side rhythm divides 28m into seven 4m modules. Recess glazing 0.35–0.60m behind the outer stone plane.
Floor edges read as restrained horizontal bands; vertical piers remain aligned through the lower body. Balcony/terrace guardrails use dark metal and clear glazing only where the composition benefits.
Ground floor is taller and more transparent at arrival, with a 2.4–3.0m deep sheltered entrance zone to investigate. No floating monumental cantilever is assumed.
Create three reusable modules: standard window bay, terrace edge, ground arrival. Use instance variation rather than unique materials per window.
Review shadow response at front, side and three-quarter angles before adding fine joints.
