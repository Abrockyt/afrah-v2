# 15 / Interaction plan
First prototype: native Begin/Skip, chapter anchors, Day/Dusk, Replay. Labels remain usable when WebGL fails. Canvas is a complementary visual, not the only source of information.

Later explorer:
1. Enter a stable camera.
2. Choose a floor by accessible list or pointer.
3. Separate floors by roughly 0.7m visually, with a clear exit/reset.
4. Choose a residence by list or canvas.
5. Present approved plan/data in normal HTML.
Keyboard list selection must highlight the same geometry. Hover must never be required to obtain information.
Unknown bedroom count, area or availability must not render as fabricated values. Touch has clear selected states, no cursor tricks. Escape closes overlays and returns focus to the invoking control.
