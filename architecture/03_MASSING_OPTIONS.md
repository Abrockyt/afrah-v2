# 03 / Three massing options
See [four-view plates](../review.html#massing). Diagrams are original volumetric studies with dimensionally consistent outer envelopes, not finished architecture. Front/side are orthographic; aerial and street are schematic oblique projections.

| Option | Envelope and silhouette | Advantages | Disadvantages | Animation | Runtime implications |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A / Courtyard Frame | 46×28×42.45m frame; 22×10m internal void | Clear perimeter, sheltered garden, strong aerial figure | 12-level narrow courtyard needs serious daylight study; dual façade doubles detail | Trace courtyard void, reveal perimeter wings | More internal faces and shadow work |
| B / Stepped Terraces | 46×28m lower body; L09 42×24, L10 38×20, L11 34×16; 42.45m total | Recognizable crown, manageable repeating modules, terrace/daylight story | Reduced upper area; drainage/privacy and transfer structure unresolved | Vertical reveal ends with increasingly smaller plates | One core and repeated façade instances; most efficient first study |
| C / Twin Volume | Two 19×28m wings separated by 8m slot; 46×28 overall; 42.45m | Strong light slot, distinct identity and camera framing | Split circulation/core strategy; slot shading and connection logic | Paired reveals, camera sightline through gap | Extra inner façade; two volume groups; more occlusion |

## Recommendation
B. Its silhouette remains legible from a single camera and on a phone, with fewer occluded surfaces than A/C. The setbacks support the LIGHT chapter without requiring dramatic movement. This is a design recommendation; usable apartments require subsequent planning.
