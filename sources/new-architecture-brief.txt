01 — ARCHITECTURAL GOAL
============================================================

Create a premium contemporary residential building suitable for a
high-end international / UK-influenced audience.

The architecture should feel:

quiet
precise
timeless
warm
modern
architectural
luxurious without being flashy
international
human-scale

- futuristic sci-fi towers
- Dubai-style exaggerated luxury
- excessive glass
- random twisting geometry
- impossible cantilevers
- generic skyscraper appearance
- all-white modern villa clichés
- excessive gold
- parametric geometry just for spectacle

The building must look believable enough that viewers can imagine it being built.
