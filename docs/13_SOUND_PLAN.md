# 13 / Sound plan
Default is silence. Show Sound on/off only once a working soundscape exists. Start audio from a deliberate gesture, preserve mute while moving between chapters and pause on hidden tabs.

Palette target: 10–12 curated assets eventually. Prototype needs at most two: quiet exterior air and a subtle dusk ambience. FORM does not need an impact or construction noise.
Later: foliage, distant city, lobby room tone, soft door, material touch. Avoid intelligible conversation, trailer booms and looping music as a substitute for atmosphere.

Master WAV 48kHz; deliver AAC/MP3 or tested Opus. Start conservative gain, leave at least 6dB peak headroom, and audition on laptop speakers and headphones. Crossfade ambience over the same two-second lighting transition. Do not claim physical sound measurement from reference pages.
Sourcing: original recording first; then asset-specific Mixkit/Freesound licence review. A catalogue listing is not an acquired asset.
